VYNTRA STORE - CUSTOMER SUPPORT FAQ

1. Delivery Policy
Standard delivery takes 3 to 5 business days.
Customers receive tracking details after their orders are shipped.
Same-day delivery is not currently available.

2. Return Policy
Customers can return eligible products within 30 days of delivery.
Products must be unused and returned in their original packaging.

3. Refund Policy
Refunds are processed within 5 to 7 business days after approval.
The refund is sent to the original payment method.

4. Payment Methods
Customers can pay using credit cards, debit cards, and UPI.
Cash on delivery is available for eligible orders.

5. Customer Support
Customers can contact support through the contact form on our website.
Our support team responds within 24 hours.

6. Warranty Policy
The warranty period varies by product.
Customers should contact support to confirm the warranty for a specific product.
